# Packet P20 — Original-objective completion gap audit

## Objective

Prove, requirement by requirement, which parts of the original SaaS affiliate objective are achieved, contradicted,
missing or externally blocked. Identify and implement any remaining credential-free work that materially shortens the
path to a public MVP, 80% autonomous operation and verified JPY 200,000 monthly net operating profit.

## Context

P1–P19 provide a credential-free control plane and keep public/business/production authority at STOP. A passing local
suite is evidence only for its declared scope and must not be treated as proof of rights, affiliate access, demand,
EPC, production isolation, publication or revenue.

## Ownership

- Integration/Release: requirement matrix, integration decision and implementation.
- Contract/Storage: evidence strength, state/replay/recovery gaps.
- Security: production-boundary and credential-isolation gaps.
- TCO/QA: market, automation and net-profit evidence sufficiency.

## Do

1. Derive exact requirements from the objective, roadmap, acceptance documents and current STOP reports.
2. Bind every requirement to direct authoritative evidence and classify it as achieved, contradicted, weak or missing.
3. Separate genuinely external inputs from local engineering still possible without credentials or external writes.
4. Implement the highest-criticality local gaps with direct counterexample tests.
5. Regenerate exact-scope schemas/fixtures and rerun blast-radius verification.

## Do not

- invent rights, affiliate approvals, demand, EPC, provider facts, production signatures or Human decisions;
- deploy, publish, apply, email, create accounts, spend, fetch unapproved sources or handle credentials;
- redefine completion as local test success.

## Expected output

`results/P20-completion-gap-audit.md` containing the evidence matrix, accepted local changes, rejected shortcuts,
external resume inputs and verification receipts.

## Verification

Focused tests for every changed boundary, deterministic schemas and STOP fixtures, full Python/Web/supply-chain checks,
workflow verification and independent Contract/Storage plus Security audit.
